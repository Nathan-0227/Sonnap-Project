# 生理狀況的常見原因：逐篇原文整理

> 狀態：2026-11-17 研究助理整理，2026-11-18 使用者審過由此寫成的句子（見 [起床小建議與自評.md](起床小建議與自評.md)「生理狀況」一節）。這份是那一節的逐篇證據。
> 對象：健康成人。不含疾病名稱、藥物、CBT-I 治療技巧。
> 書目一律由 Europe PMC（鏡像 PubMed 書目資料）逐篇核對第一作者、年份、期刊、卷期頁、DOI、PMID。
> 「讀到」欄：**全文**＝讀過 PMC／期刊全文頁；**摘要**＝只讀到 PubMed 摘要；**書目**＝連摘要都沒有。
> 原文短引都是英文原句；從全文頁取得的句子是請抓取工具逐字摘出的，正式採用前建議再對一次 PDF。

## 證據等級代號

| 代號 | 意思 |
|---|---|
| **MA** | 統合分析／系統性回顧 |
| **RCT/X** | 隨機或交叉實驗（實驗室，可談因果，但樣本常小） |
| **OBS** | 觀察性研究（只能寫 *is linked with*，不能寫 *causes*） |

## 2. 各狀況細節

### ① 睡眠時長偏短（低於 7 小時）

評分依據：Hirshkowitz 2015（NSF）——*"Seven to 9 hours is recommended for young adults and adults, and 7-8 hours of sleep is recommended for older adults."*（摘要）

**原因 1：咖啡因——Drake 2013（RCT/X，全文）**
- 設計：400 mg 咖啡因 vs 安慰劑，分別在睡前 0、3、6 小時服用，在家用睡眠監測。
- 總睡眠：三個時間點都顯著減少約 1.1–1.2 小時。*"Even at 6 hours, caffeine reduced sleep by more than 1 hour."*
- 結論：*"caffeine taken 6 hours before bedtime has important disruptive effects on sleep."*
- 可以做：作者明寫支持 *"sleep hygiene recommendations to refrain from substantial caffeine use"*，至少睡前 6 小時。
- ⚠️ 400 mg 是大劑量（約 4 杯咖啡）；小劑量的效果這篇沒測。

**原因 2：睡前不久的高強度運動——Leota 2025（OBS，全文）＋ Stutz 2019（MA，摘要）**
- Leota：14,689 人、4,084,354 個夜晚的 WHOOP 資料，個人內比較。*"later exercise timing and higher exercise strain are associated with delayed sleep onset, shorter sleep duration, lower sleep quality, higher nocturnal resting heart rate, and lower nocturnal heart rate variability."*
- 數字（入睡前 2 小時結束、最高強度 vs 輕度）：*"Engaging in maximal exercise instead of light exercise is associated with a 5.4% (22.2 minute) shorter sleep duration"*；入睡延後 36.0 分鐘。
- *"Regardless of strain, exercise bouts ending ≥4 hours before sleep onset are not associated with changes in sleep."*
- ⚠️ Leota 的「強度」是用心率區間算的 SHRZS，**不是 WHOOP Strain 分數**；觀察性，只能說 *is linked with*。全文 Discussion 有一句把 22.2 分鐘寫成「增加」，與 Results 矛盾，引用時以 Results 為準。
- Stutz 2019 統合分析（23 篇）結論相反方向的保護性訊息：*"the studies reviewed here do not support the hypothesis that evening exercise negatively affects sleep, in fact rather the opposite. However, sleep-onset latency, total sleep time, and SE might be impaired after vigorous exercise ending ≤ 1 h before bedtime."*
- → 建議措辭：不是「晚上別運動」，而是「劇烈運動早點結束」。

**原因 3：熄燈後用手機——Exelmans 2016（OBS 橫斷、自評，摘要）**
- 844 名成人問卷。*"Sending/receiving text messages and/or phone calls after lights out significantly predicted respondents' scores on the PSQI, particularly longer sleep latency, worse sleep efficiency, more sleep disturbance and more daytime dysfunction."*
- ⚠️ **對「總睡眠時長」只在年長者成立**：*"it was related to an earlier rise time and shorter sleep duration among older respondents (≥ 60.15 years old and ≥ 66.4 years old respectively)"*；年輕者是起床較晚。所以它支持「入睡慢、睡眠效率差」，**對 <7 小時這條的支持很弱**。

**原因 4：作息不規律——Phillips 2017（OBS，全文）**
- 61 名大學生 30 天日誌。不規律者**24 小時總睡眠沒有比較少**：*"There was no significant difference in average daily sleep duration between the Irregular (7.16 ± 0.64 h) and Regular (7.27 ± 0.59 h) groups"*；但夜間睡得少（表 1：6.61 vs 5.01 h），白天補（小睡每週 3.02 vs 0.75 次）。
- 摘要：*"irregular sleep and light exposure patterns in college students are associated with delayed circadian rhythms"*。
- → 對手錶只量「夜間那一段」的 App 而言，作息不規律**確實會表現成夜間睡得短**，但措辭要寫「is linked with」，且樣本是大學生。

**原因 5：熱的寢室——Okamoto-Mizuno 2012（REV，全文）**
- *"In real-life situations where bedding and clothing are used, heat exposure increases wakefulness and decreases slow wave sleep and rapid eye movement sleep."*
- 可以做：*"if air conditioning use is limited, then it should be used during the initial segment of sleep"*。

**原因 6：睡前擔心未完成的事——Scullin 2018（RCT 組間，全文）**
- 57 名 18–30 歲，睡前寫 5 分鐘。待辦清單組入睡 15.82 分 vs 已完成清單組 25.09 分（PSG）。
- ⚠️ **總睡眠沒有差**（498.53 vs 492.40 分，p = 0.67），WASO、N3、REM 也都沒有差。所以它支持「入睡較快」，**不支持「睡得比較久」**。
- 可以做：*"individuals may derive benefit from writing a very specific to-do list for 5 min at bedtime"*。

**原因 7：時差——Sack 2010（REV，只有書目）**
- NEJM 全文抓取被擋（403），Europe PMC 也沒有摘要。**內容未讀到，不能引用任何主張。**

### ② WASO（入睡後清醒）偏多

**咖啡因——Drake 2013（全文）**
- *"The amount of wake time during sleep was also increased with all 3 caffeine administration times, reaching statistical significance for the 6 h (+8 min) and 3 h (+27.6 min) conditions."*（睡前 0 小時那組不顯著）

**酒精——Ebrahim 2013（REV，摘要）**
- *"At all dosages, alcohol causes a reduction in sleep onset latency, a more consolidated first half sleep and an increase in sleep disruption in the second half of sleep."*
- ⚠️ 反直覺：前半夜反而「更連續」，打斷集中在後半夜。

**熱環境——Okamoto-Mizuno 2012（全文）**
- *"Humid heat exposure further increases wakefulness, decreases REM and SWS"*；*"Heat-related sleep disruptions do not adapt even after 5 days of continuous daytime and nocturnal heat exposure"*。

**噪音——Basner 2014（REV，全文）**
- 反覆被噪音喚起會造成 *"reduced deep (slow-wave) and rapid eye movement sleep"*，另列 *"early awakenings"* 與 *"delayed sleep onset"*。

**晚間運動的調節因子——Stutz 2019（MA，摘要）**
- *"A higher level of physical stress (exercise intensity relative to baseline physical activity) was associated with lower SE (- 3.2 pp; p = 0.036) and more WASO (+ 21.9 min; p = 0.044)."*
- ⚠️ 同一段摘要：*"All significant moderating effects disappeared after removal of one study."* → **證據脆弱，只能當旁證。**

**白天運動（保護方向）——Kredlow 2015（MA，摘要）**
- *"acute exercise has small beneficial effects on total sleep time, sleep onset latency, sleep efficiency, stage 1 sleep, and slow wave sleep, a moderate beneficial effect on wake time after sleep onset"*。
- ⚠️ 摘要沒有效果量數字（Hedges' g），全文付費牆讀不到。**草稿裡不要寫任何數字。**

### ③ 深睡（N3／SWS）比例偏低

**咖啡因——Drake 2013（全文）**
- *"Reductions in the duration of slow wave sleep were observed for all 3 caffeine conditions, but reached significance only for administration at bedtime and 6 h before bedtime."*

**熱環境、噪音**：同上（Okamoto-Mizuno 2012；Basner 2014）。

**白天小睡——Dijk 2009（REV，全文）**
- 小睡 *"led to a reduction in the level of SWS or SWA in the initial part of subsequent nocturnal sleep."*
- ⚠️ 原本清單上的 Milner & Cote 2009 只讀到摘要，摘要只談小睡的好處（長度、時段、年齡），**沒有提到對當晚睡眠的影響**，不能拿來支持這一條。

**睡眠恆定（前一晚／白天醒得久 → 深睡多；反之少）——Dijk 2009（全文）、Brunner 1990/1993（摘要）**
- Dijk：*"Recovery from sleep deprivation is accompanied by a marked increase in NREM sleep duration and enhancement of SWA."*；*"SWA declines during all sleep episodes, almost independently of the circadian phase."*
- Brunner 1993（連續 4 晚只睡 4 小時）：*"Slow wave sleep was increased only in the first recovery night."*
- → App 措辭含意：前一晚睡很多、或白天小睡過，當晚深睡比例低**可能是正常的恆定調節，不是需要改的事**。
- 年齡：*"older people, and men in particular, have far less SWS/SWA than young people."*（Dijk 2009）——不是改得了的原因，但個人基線要考慮。

**⚠️ 反直覺：讓深睡變多的東西**
- 酒精：*"The majority of studies, across dose, age and gender, confirm an increase in slow wave sleep (SWS) in the first half of the night relative to baseline values."*；*"Total night SWS is increased at high alcohol doses"*（Ebrahim 2013）。→ **深睡多不等於睡得好，也不能建議喝酒**。
- 晚間運動：*"evening exercise significantly increased rapid eye movement latency (+ 7.7 min; p = 0.032) and slow-wave sleep (+ 1.3 percentage points [pp]; p = 0.041)"*（Stutz 2019）。
- 晚餐吃晚：Duan 2021 *"LD was associated with deeper sleep in the beginning of the night and lighter sleep in the latter part of the night"*（頻譜層級，傳統分期沒有差）。

### ④ REM 比例偏低

**睡得短 → 先少 REM**
- IOM 2006（REV，全文）：*"In normal adults, REM sleep increases as the night progresses and is longest in the last one-third of the sleep episode."*；*"Sleep stages 3 and 4 are collectively referred to as slow-wave sleep (SWS), most of which occurs during the first third of the night."*（該段落引 Carskadon & Dement 2005）
- Brunner 1990（RCT/X，摘要）：限制成只睡前 4 小時時，*"stages 1 and 2 and REM sleep were reduced, while slow wave sleep (SWS; stages 3 and 4) was not significantly affected."* 恢復期 *"REM sleep showed a rebound in R1 and R2."*
- Brunner 1993（摘要）：*"Sleep restriction curtailed sleep stages 1 and 2 as well as rapid eye movement (REM) sleep, but left slow wave sleep largely unaffected."*
- → 最直接的「可以做」：**睡滿時長**。REM 偏低的晚上要先看是不是同時睡得短。
- ⚠️ Carskadon & Dement 教科書章節原文沒有讀到（PDF 抓取為空），所以引 IOM 2006 這份讀到全文的二手彙整，並註明它的出處。

**酒精——Ebrahim 2013（摘要）**
- *"Total night REM sleep percentage is decreased in the majority of studies at moderate and high doses with no clear trend apparent at low doses."*
- *"The onset of the first REM sleep period is significantly delayed at all doses"*；前半夜 REM 減少只在高劑量顯著。

**熱環境、噪音**：同上。

**睡前用發光螢幕——Chang 2015（RCT/X，全文）**
- 12 人住院、睡前 4 小時（18:00–22:00）用 iPad 最高亮度讀書 vs 紙本，固定睡 8 小時。
- *"Participants also had significantly less rapid eye movement (REM) sleep following the LE-eBook condition (109.04 ± 26.25 min vs. 120.86 ± 25.32 min in the print-book condition; P = 0.03)."*
- 入睡多約 10 分鐘（25.65 vs 15.75）。*"There was no difference between conditions in TST, sleep efficiency, or the duration of non-REM sleep."*
- ⚠️ 情境極端（4 小時、最高亮度、對照組是昏暗燈光），n=12，不宜放大。

**傍晚高強度運動——Frimpong 2021（MA，摘要）**
- 15 篇、194 人：*"Acute evening HIE ending 0.5-4 h before bedtime decreased rapid eye movement sleep (-2.34%; p = 0.002) compared with a no-exercise control. No other significant sleep changes occurred."*
- 同時：*"acute evening HIE performed 2-4 h before bedtime does not disrupt nighttime sleep"*。

**生病——Besedovsky 2019（REV，全文部分）**
- *"animal and human studies have repeatedly shown a concomitant suppression of REM sleep"*（免疫活化時）。
- 輕微免疫反應可能增加 NREM，較強反應與發燒則打斷睡眠：*"NREM sleep enhancing or intensifying effects are restricted to times of very mild host response activation"*、*"whereas stronger responses characterized by large cytokine increases and fever caused sleep disruption"*。
- 感冒（rhinovirus）：*"reduced total sleep time and reduced sleep efficiency in the active phase of the illness"*。

### ⑤ 睡眠期間心率比自己平常高

**酒精——Pietilä 2018（OBS，全文）**
- 第一作者 **Pietilä J**（核對過）；JMIR Ment Health 2018;5(1):e23。
- 樣本：4,098 名芬蘭員工（2,287 女）、12,411 個記錄日，個人內比較（有喝 vs 沒喝的日子），心率取自主回報就寢後 30 分鐘起的 3 小時。
- 劑量效應：*"HR was increased by 1.4 bpm with low, 4.0 bpm with moderate, and 8.7 bpm with high alcohol intake."*（低 ≤0.25 g/kg、中 >0.25–0.75、高 >0.75 g/kg 純酒精）
- 摘要：*"Alcohol intake was dose-dependently associated with increased sympathetic regulation, decreased parasympathetic regulation, and insufficient recovery."*
- ⚠️ 觀察性、飲酒量自述、**沒有量睡眠分期**（那 3 小時是「最可能是深睡的時段」，作者引文獻推定）。作者 Discussion 用了 *caused*，我們只寫 *is linked with*。

**晚間高強度運動——Leota 2025（OBS，全文）、Myllymäki 2011（X，摘要）**
- Leota：入睡前 2 小時結束的最高強度 vs 輕度，*"associated with a 6.8% (3.9 beats/min) increase in RHR"*；≥4 小時前結束則無關聯。
- Myllymäki：11 名體能好的年輕人，力竭騎車在睡前約 2 小時 13 分結束。*"HR was higher after the exercise day than the control day (54±7 versus 51±7, P<0.01), and especially during the first three sleeping hours."* 但 *"vigorous late-night exercise does not disturb sleep quality."*
- → 措辭：心率高而睡眠分期沒變，是**可能的**情況，不要因心率高就說「睡不好」。

**生病／發燒——Karjalainen 1986（OBS，摘要）、Radin 2020（OBS，摘要）**
- Karjalainen：27 名急性發燒感染的年輕男性，24 小時心電圖。*"When the temperature rose by 1 degree C, the heart rate increased on the average by 8.5 beats per minute. During the febrile period, the heart rate remained high, even during sleep."*
- Radin（Fitbit，47,249 人）：*"Acute infections can cause an individual to have an elevated resting heart rate (RHR)"*，用 RHR 升高＋睡眠增加追蹤類流感趨勢。（族群層級，不是個人診斷工具）
- ⚠️ Besedovsky 2019 讀到的部分**沒有**睡眠期間心率的資料；生病→心率這一條改由上面兩篇支持。
- App 措辭：只能說「生病時心率常會比較高」，不能推論使用者生病（紅線：不給疾病名稱）。

**噪音——Basner 2014（全文）**
- 低音量就可能引起 *"physiological reactions during sleep including autonomic, motor, and cortical arousals"*，例子是 *"tachycardia, body movements, and awakenings"*；且自主神經喚起 *"have been shown to habituate to a much lesser degree to noise"*。
- ⚠️ 原文沒有明說「沒醒也會心跳加快」。

**晚餐吃很晚**：見第 3 節，沒找到夠好的證據。

### ⑥ 白天步數／身體活動與當晚睡眠

- **Kredlow 2015（MA，66 篇，摘要）**：單次運動 → TST、SOL、SE、SWS 小幅改善、WASO 中度改善；規律運動 → TST、SE 小幅、SOL 小到中、主觀睡眠品質中度改善。*"Effects were moderated by sex, age, baseline physical activity level of participants, as well as exercise type, time of day, duration, and adherence."*
- **Atoui 2021（MA，33 篇逐日研究，摘要）——比 Kredlow 更接近「今天 vs 今晚」**：
  - *"The qualitative and quantitative analyses of included studies did not support a bidirectional daily association between sleep outcomes and physical activity."*
  - *"Daytime physical activity was associated with lower total sleep time the following night at an inter-person level with a small effect size."*（注意：是**人與人之間**、方向是「較少」）
  - 反方向（昨晚睡眠 → 隔天活動）才有小關聯：睡眠品質、效率、WASO。
- **Leota 2025**：輕／中強度、入睡前 >2 小時結束的運動，睡眠與沒運動的晚上相似；≥4 小時前結束的任何強度都無關聯。
- → **對 App 的意義**：「今天步數少」**不適合**當成「昨晚／今晚睡不好」的原因——逐日證據不支持。可以保留「規律運動 is linked with 較好的睡眠」這種長期說法（Kredlow），但不要做逐晚歸因。

## 3. 沒找到夠好的證據

| 主張 | 情形 |
|---|---|
| **晚餐吃很晚 → 夜間心率高** | 沒找到健康成人、量睡眠心率的對照研究。最接近的 RCT（Gu 2020，n=20，22:00 vs 18:00 晚餐）：*"LD did not affect sleep architecture, but increased plasma cortisol."* 其事後分析（Duan 2021）：*"Conventional sleep stages were similar between the 2 visits."* 一個網路摘要提到「晚餐後最高心率 +1.54 bpm」，但找不到原始研究，**不採用**。 |
| **晚餐吃很晚 → 睡不好** | 上述 RCT 結論相反：*"did not result in significant adverse changes in overnight sleep architecture"*。只有觀察性研究支持「吃晚睡不好」，本次未找到可用的一手來源。 |
| Kredlow 2015 的效果量數字 | 全文付費牆，摘要只寫 small／moderate。**不寫數字**。對 REM 摘要只說 *"a small effect"*，**方向不明**。 |
| Sack 2010（時差）內容 | NEJM 403、無摘要。**未讀，不引用**。 |
| Milner & Cote 2009 對當晚睡眠的影響 | 摘要沒提，全文付費牆。改用 Dijk 2009 支持「小睡減少當晚前段深睡」。 |

| 熱環境 → 心率 | 未查到（Okamoto-Mizuno 2012 讀到的部分沒有心率數字）。 |
